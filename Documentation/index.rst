==================
EOLA documentation
==================

End-Of-Life Attestation (EOLA) is an open protocol for keeping a game
playable after its publisher stops operating it. This is the top level of
its documentation, grouped by what the reader does with EOLA.

Specification
=============

What an implementation of EOLA does, in drafts published with this
documentation:

* `EOLA Architecture <spec/draft-wilczek-eola-architecture.html>`_ --
  roles, keys, documents and the life cycle of a game.
